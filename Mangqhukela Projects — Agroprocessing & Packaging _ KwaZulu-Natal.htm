About us

A KwaZulu-Natal enterprise rooted in land, community and quality.

We partner with smallholder farmers to source, process and package staples that families rely on every day — sugar beans, soya, spinach and spice blends under our Amandla brand.

Our mission is simple: empower local farmers, add real value through agroprocessing, and drive rural economic development with every packet sold.

Vision

To become a leading agroprocessing brand in Southern Africa.

Mission

Affordable, nutritious food for every community we serve.
